## Content Strategy

### Article Structure (Persuasive Framework)

Each article follows a proven conversion structure:

1. **Hook/Problem Agitation** - Identify pain point
2. **CTA #1 (Quiz)** - Early engagement opportunity
3. **Educational Content** - Provide value and build authority
4. **Social Proof** - Include quotes and real-world examples
5. **CTA #2 (Strategy Call)** - Primary conversion
6. **Secondary CTA** - Quiz option for non-committal visitors
7. **Conclusion** - Reinforce urgency and value

### CTA Placement Strategy

- **Primary CTA:** Schedule-a-call (highest intent)
- **Secondary CTA:** Quiz (lower friction, lead generation)
- **Placement:** 
  - After problem introduction (30% scroll)
  - After solution presentation (70% scroll)
  - End of article (100% scroll)
