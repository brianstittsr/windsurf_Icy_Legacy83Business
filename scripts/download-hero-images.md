## Quick Download Instructions

### Option 1: Use the Admin Panel (Recommended)
1. Go to `/portal/admin/hero`
2. Click "Edit" on each slide
3. Go to "Design" tab → "Background Image"
4. Click "Search Stock" tab
5. Enter search terms from above
6. Click any image to auto-save and select

### Option 2: Manual Download
1. Visit the Pexels/Unsplash links above
2. Download your preferred images (1920x1080 or larger)
3. Go to `/portal/admin/images`
4. Upload with category "hero"
5. Return to hero management and select from "Library"

## Image Requirements
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1920x1080 (landscape)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Style:** Professional, diverse people, modern business setting
- **Color Tone:** Warm, professional, matching brand colors
- **License:** Free for commercial use (Pexels/Unsplash standard license)
